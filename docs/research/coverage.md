# Ballot coverage and editorial review

Reviewed September 25, 2026.

Carolina Beach is in precinct FP08. The New Hanover County jurisdiction maps place FP08 in U.S. House District 7, NC Senate District 7, NC House District 19 and Superior Court District 6C. Of the seven county sample ballots, B0002 matches those legislative districts and does not include the District 6B superior-court contest. The guide therefore follows B0002.

The candidate roster was transcribed from the county's official B0002 sample ballot and checked against the State Board's current candidate-list page. Ballot order is not reproduced: candidates are alphabetical within each race. All four referendum questions on B0002 are included.

| Coverage | Races |
| --- | --- |
| Federal | U.S. Senate; U.S. House District 7 |
| State judicial | Supreme Court Seat 1; Court of Appeals Seats 1–3 |
| Legislature | NC Senate District 7; NC House District 19 |
| Local justice | District Court District 6 Seats 1–4; District Attorney District 6; Clerk of Superior Court; Sheriff |
| County | Commissioners; Board of Education; Soil and Water Conservation District Supervisor |
| Referenda | Three state constitutional amendments; New Hanover County school bonds |

Regular Carolina Beach mayor and town-council contests are excluded because the county's term schedule lists those elections in 2027 and 2029, not 2026. No Carolina Beach special municipal contest appears on B0002.

## Editorial limits

The official ballot establishes candidacy, but many lower-profile campaigns do not publish detailed, readily verifiable platforms. Where a candidate's public information was sparse, the guide says so instead of inferring views from party affiliation. Judicial profiles point readers to the State Board's official judicial guide. Candidate photographs are not bundled in the initial release because a consistent set with clear reuse permission could not be verified for every candidate; the design uses prominent initials as an honest fallback. This is a material limitation and should be revisited when campaigns provide reusable press photos.
